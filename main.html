12 Jyotirlingas of Lord Shiva


The 12 Jyotirlingas are the most sacred shrines dedicated to Lord Shiva in Hinduism. The word Jyotirlinga is made up of two words: Jyoti, meaning light, and Linga, representing Lord Shiva. Together, Jyotirlinga means the divine pillar of light that represents the infinite nature of Lord Shiva. According to Hindu beliefs, Lord Shiva manifested himself as a Jyotirlinga at twelve different places across India. These twelve temples are considered highly sacred, and devotees visit them to seek blessings, peace, prosperity and spiritual enlightenment.

  1. Kedarnath – Uttarakhand
  2. Mahakaleshwar – Ujjain, Madhya Pradesh
  3. Kashi Vishwanath – Varanasi, Uttar Pradesh
  4. Somnath – Gujarat
  5. Rameshwaram – Tamil Nadu
  • Aloo Ke Gutke – Kedarnath region
  • Bhutte Ka Kees – Ujjain
  • Banarasi Tamatar Chaat – Varanasi
  • Kathiyawadi Thali – Somnath
  • Traditional South Indian Meals – Rameswaram
